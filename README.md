# 📊 Task 4 – Dashboard Design

## 🎯 Objective
Design an interactive dashboard for business stakeholders to analyze sales performance.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI
- Kaggle Sales Dataset

## 📌 Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards (Revenue, Orders, Profit, Profit Margin)
- Revenue Trend Over Time
- Profit Margin Trend
- Revenue by Product Category
- Profit by Product Category
- Revenue by Country
- Interactive Slicers (Year, Category, Country)

## 📊 Key Insights
- Bikes category generates the highest revenue and profit
- United States contributes the highest overall revenue
- Profit margin remains around 30–35%
- Revenue shows seasonal fluctuations
